What it does

One app for every count on the floor

The three things that go missing in a convenience store — cash, lottery, and high-shrink stock — counted the same trustworthy way.

Cash drawers

Opening and closing counts with a live denomination counter, and instant over/short — no adding up by hand.

Scratch-offs & lottery

Pack lifecycle, barcode scanning, and a flexible settlement-file reconcile that flags discrepancies.

Inventory

Track just the high-shrink items that matter; counts off by your unit threshold get flagged like a cash short.

A real trust model

Every entry is signed and append-only. Counts can't be quietly edited or deleted — the history stays honest.

Blind counts & verify

Count the real drawer, not to a target. A manager verifies — never their own count.

Reports for the record

Any period — day to year, fiscal-year, or custom. PDF, CSV, and print, with side-by-side multi-location compare.

Time clock & schedule

Append-only punches, payroll CSV, weekly rosters, availability, shift swaps, and open shifts.

Installable & offline

Add it to the home screen like a native app. It opens full-screen and works offline for the basics.

Why it's trustworthy

Numbers you can stand behind

DuoCount's whole design is one promise: every number has a name, and nothing is edited after the fact.

Signed

Each count is stamped with who entered it and when — automatically, every time.

Append-only

Records can't be altered or removed from the app — enforced by the database rules, not just convention.

Countersigned

A second set of eyes verifies. Anything off by more than your threshold is flagged with a reason.

Start counting with confidence

Load the built-in sample data and click through every screen in a couple of minutes — no setup required.

sponsored by, or endorsed by** any state or national lottery, gaming commission,
or any product manufacturer or brand. Where the app helps reconcile a

any lottery authority.

## Not financial, tax, accounting, or legal advice

DuoCount helps you **record and export** what your staff count. Its reports and
exports are for your own record-keeping (accountant, franchise, tax, audit) and
are **not** financial, tax, accounting, or legal advice. The app does not verify
that the numbers entered are correct, complete, or compliant with any regulation.
Consult a qualified professional for advice, and treat the app's output as an
input to — not a substitute for — your own books.

## Accuracy & the honest-count model

The app records what signed-in staff enter and flags variances against the
thresholds you set. It reduces opportunities for undetected error, but it cannot
guarantee that any individual count is accurate or that variances are always
caught. It is a control aid, not a guarantee.

## Regulated goods

If your store sells age-restricted or otherwise regulated goods (tobacco,
lottery, etc.), complying with the rules that govern their sale, recording, and
reconciliation is **your responsibility**. DuoCount does not enforce or certify
regulatory compliance.

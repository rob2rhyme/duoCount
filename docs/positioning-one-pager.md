# DuoCount — Positioning One-Pager

## One line
The trust layer for cash-heavy retail teams: drawer and scratch-off counts with a name, a timestamp, and a second set of eyes on every number — no POS switch, no hardware.

**The name.** DuoCount = the two-person, countersigned count — the one mechanic every feature shares. (Screened for conflicts: no competing software found; duocount.com is squatted by a spam site, so launch as duocount.app / getduocount.com. USPTO + app-store clearance still pending.)

## Target customer
Independent smoke shops, convenience stores, gas stations, and liquor stores that sell lottery; 1–5 locations; currently tracking counts on paper logs or spreadsheets; unwilling to replace their POS. The **buyer** is the owner; the **users** are the entire staff — which is unusual in this category and part of the pitch: it protects employees, not just owners.

## The problem
Paper lottery logs cost roughly 15 minutes per shift per employee plus manager review [1], and industry estimates put the average lottery-selling store's shrink at about $5,000 a year — 40–50% of it attributed to employee theft [2] (for economy-wide context, see [3]). Meanwhile, shortage disputes with no clean record poison staff trust: employees get blamed without evidence, and owners suspect without proof.

## Positioning statement
For independent lottery retailers still counting on paper, **DuoCount** is a team accountability app where every cash, scratch-off, and inventory count is signed, timestamped, verified by a second person, and permanently un-editable — so the same record protects the owner and the employee. Unlike lottery specialists, it also covers cash drawers, disputes, team roles, scratch-off ticket-sequence theft protection at every shift boundary, and a full time-clock and scheduling suite. Unlike free phone counter apps, it's multi-user with manager verification and per-location controls. Unlike POS-native tools, it works alongside whatever register the store already has.

## Pricing (suggested placement)
- **Free** — 1 location, 2 drawers, 30-day history. The wedge to replace the paper log.
- **Pro — ~$29/location/month** — unlimited drawers and history, blind counts, variance flags, disputes, EOD PDF, daily email digest.
- Anchors: less than half of LottoShield's entry price; roughly 7% of the average store's annual lottery shrink; in the same band stores already pay for lottery-only bolt-ons ($30–50/mo).

## Proof points to sell with
1. Industry best practice already demands exactly this: witnessed counts co-signed by both parties, and cashier-level variance tracking as the most effective small-store accountability tool [4].
2. Sequential ticket math is self-auditing — end # − start # must match the drawer.
3. Append-only records + manager verification = documentation that stands up in a dispute, for either side.

## Honest risks
1. **Distribution beats product.** The buyers are fragmented, low-tech independents; reaching them (associations, owner communities, referrals) is the real cost.
2. **Thin moat.** The edge is the combination and the trust framing, not proprietary tech; POS vendors could bundle it.
3. **Scanning — gap now closed.** Specialists led with barcode scanning while we took typed ticket numbers; phone-camera barcode scanning has since shipped (scratch-off packs, inventory items, pack registry, and admin), so the pack/ticket numbers are scan-fillable with no add-on hardware. The residual work here is awareness and execution, not a missing capability.

## Go-to-market wedge
Lead with **"Replace the paper lottery log."** Channels: state lottery retailer associations, c-store and smoke-shop owner groups, and the POS-agnostic angle ("keep your register, fix your counts"). Land single stores on Free, convert on reports + multi-location, expand as they add locations.
